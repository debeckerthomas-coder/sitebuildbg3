# The Bardadin: The Maestro of Divine Smite

*(10 College of Swords Bard / 2 Paladin)*

The "Bardadin" (also known as the Smite Swords Bard) is one of the most terrifying melee builds in the game. Unlike a pure Paladin who quickly runs out of steam due to a lack of Spell Slots, a level 10 Bard provides a massive magical reserve. You can use a *Slashing Flourish* to hit two enemies at once, and trigger a <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> of 4th Level Spell on **each** target simultaneously.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

To maximise the effectiveness of your melee weapons and spells, we build around Dexterity and Charisma, using "Finesse" weapons.

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Orc (the Savage Attacks passive adds an extra damage die on every critical hit, perfect for Smites) or Zariel Tiefling (free Smites).
- **Background:** Charlatan or Entertainer.

**27-Point Allocation:**

- **Strength: 8** (Unnecessary, we use Finesse weapons)
- **Dexterity: 16** (Primary attack stat, Armour Class, Initiative)
- **Constitution: 14** (Hit Points)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Dump stat)
- **Wisdom: 10** (To avoid penalties)
- **Charisma: 17** (Primary spellcasting stat. +1 from the Hag = 18)

---

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

The early game plays like a standard Bard. The real carnage begins at level 8.

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_bardadin">

- **Levels 1 to 6: Bard.** Choose the **College of Swords** at level 3. This grants access to defensive and *Slashing Flourishes* in melee. At level 4, take the **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Dexterity or +2 Charisma). At level 6, you gain your Extra Attack.
- **Levels 7 to 8: Paladin (1 to 2).** The key multiclass. At Paladin level 1, you gain proficiency with heavy armour and shields (if you started as Paladin; otherwise stick to medium armour). At level 2, you unlock a Fighting Style (*Dueling* or *Defence*) and, most importantly, <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" />.
- **Levels 9 to 12: Bard (7 to 10).** Back to Bard to accumulate high-level Spell Slots.
- **Overall Level 12 (Bard 10):** You unlock *Magical Secrets*. This is where the build becomes divine: choose the Paladin spell <CodexTooltipById id="banishing_smite" text="Banishing Smite" /> (5d10 force damage) and <CodexTooltipById id="counterspell" text="Counterspell" />.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

The Bardadin is capable of annihilating any boss and paralysing their minions in the same turn.

1. **The Opener:** Use your Main Action to perform a *Slashing Flourish (Melee)*. If two enemies are side by side, you hit them both.
2. **The Divine Discharge:** On the Reaction screen, apply a 4th Level Spell <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> to the first target, and another to the second. (Yes, this is legal).
3. **The Buff:** Your melee attacks have charged your *<Rule term="casque_acuite">Helmet of Arcane Acuity</Rule>*. Your spells now have a 100% success rate.
4. **Control or Execution (Bonus Action):** - *Option A:* Use the *<Rule term="anneau_gredin">Ring of the Mystic Scoundrel</Rule>* to cast <CodexTooltipById id="hold_monster" text="Hold Monster" /> on the surviving boss.
   - *Option B:* If you are using Duellist's Prerogative, make an extra melee attack to finish them off with another Divine Smite.
