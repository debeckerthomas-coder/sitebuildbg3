# The Control Bard: The Master of Honour Mode

*(10 College of Swords Bard / 1 Fighter / 1 Wizard)*

The Control Bard is the reason Honour Mode has a reputation for being "actually easy." Fire 4 arrows, stack Arcane Acuity to the moon, then Bonus Action a Command spell that literally cannot fail. Every boss on screen skips their turn. 100% success rate. No RNG. Just pure cheese.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

- **Optimal Race:** Wood Elf (for mobility) or Halfling (to reroll Critical Failures of 1).
- **Background:** Charlatan or Urchin.

**27-Point Allocation:**

- **Strength: 8** - **Dexterity: 16** (Bow damage, Armour Class, Initiative)
- **Constitution: 14** (Concentration)
- **Intelligence: 8** - **Wisdom: 10** - **Charisma: 17** (Primary stat. +1 from the Hag = 18)

---

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_bard">

- **Levels 1 to 6: Bard.** Choose the **College of Swords** at level 3. This grants access to *Slashing Flourish* (two arrows on the same target). At level 4, take the **Sharpshooter** feat.
- **Level 7: Fighter (1).** Crucial multiclass for the *Archery* Fighting Style (+2 to hit).
- **Level 8: Wizard (1).** Allows learning spells from scrolls: <CodexTooltipById id="shield_spell" text="Shield" />, <CodexTooltipById id="magic_missile" text="Magic Missile" />, <CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" />.
- **Levels 9 to 12: Bard (7 to 10).** **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Charisma). At level 10, *Magical Secrets*: <CodexTooltipById id="command" text="Command" /> and <CodexTooltipById id="counterspell" text="Counterspell" />.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

1. **Main Action — stack acuity:** Two ranged *Slashing Flourishes* = 4 arrows. Each hit procs the Helmet.
2. **Acuity goes brrr:** 4 weapon hits = 4 stacks = +8 to your spell DC. Nothing resists you now.
3. **Bonus Action — lock the board:** Ring of the Mystic Scoundrel lets you cast <CodexTooltipById id="command" text="Command (Halt)" /> upcast on every enemy.
4. **GG:** 100% failure rate for them. Every boss skips their turn. You win.
