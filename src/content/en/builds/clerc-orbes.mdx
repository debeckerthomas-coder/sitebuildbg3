# The Radiating Orb Cleric: The Aura of Blindness

*(1 Storm Sorcerer / 11 Light Domain Cleric)*

The Radiating Orb build doesn't kill bosses — it makes them completely useless. Every time you deal radiant damage, you stack "Radiating Orbs" on nearby enemies. Each stack = -1 to their attack rolls. In one turn you can slap 10 stacks on a boss, and suddenly it can't hit anything. Your party becomes unkillable. It's the ultimate support cheese.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

The Sorcerer dip is pure min-max: CON save proficiency (so you never drop Spirit Guardians) and access to *Shield*. One level, massive value.

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Elf (Wood or High) for bonus movement or sleep Immunity, or Duergar (for at-will invisibility).
- **Background:** Acolyte or Sage.

**27-Point Allocation:**

- **Strength: 8** (Unnecessary)
- **Dexterity: 14** (To maximise Armour Class with medium armour)
- **Constitution: 16** (Hit Points and unbreakable Concentration)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Dump stat)
- **Wisdom: 17** (Primary stat for Cleric spells. +1 from the Hag or a feat = 18)
- **Charisma: 10**

---

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

Before level 6 you're a regular Cleric. After level 6 (Cleric 5), you unlock *Spirit Guardians* and become a walking lawnmower that shreds everything in a 3m radius.

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_lightcleric">

- **Level 1: Sorcerer (Storm Sorcery).** Why Storm? For repositioning. Casting a spell of 1st Level or higher lets you fly 3m (10ft) as a Bonus Action without provoking an Opportunity Attack (Tempestuous Magic). It is a short hop, not full flight, but useful for minor positioning adjustments. Take the <CodexTooltipById id="shield_spell" text="Shield" /> and <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" /> spells.
- **Levels 2 to 12: Cleric (Light Domain).** The Light Domain grants access to *Radiance of the Dawn* (a massive radiant AoE) and *Warding Flare* (a Reaction to impose Disadvantage on an enemy attack).
- **Level 5 (Cleric 4):** Take the **War Caster** feat. Combined with your Constitution proficiency, you will almost never lose Concentration on *Spirit Guardians*.
- **Level 9 (Cleric 8):** Take the **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Wisdom).
- **Level 11 (Cleric 10):** You unlock Divine Intervention. Save it for a legendary weapon or a full emergency heal on an endgame boss.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

You don't snipe from the back. You run straight into the enemy blob and make them all useless.

1. **Turn 1 — Spirit Guardians up:** Cast *Spirit Guardians* (Radiant, obviously). This is your lawnmower.
2. **Hop in:** Tempestuous Magic procs — 3m (10ft) Bonus Action hop, no opportunity attacks. Use it to nudge into the center of the pack, then walk the rest of the way in.
3. **The chain reaction:** Every enemy in your aura takes radiant damage. <Rule term="armure_lumineuse">Luminous Armour</Rule> procs → wave of Orbs. Gloves proc → more Orbs. Ring procs → even more Orbs. One pass = 4 to 8 stacks of Radiating Orbs on everything nearby (that's -4 to -8 on their attack rolls).
4. **Turn 2 — finish them:** *Radiance of the Dawn* — 9m radius, ignores cover, more radiant damage, more Orb stacks. The fight is mathematically won. They literally can't hit you anymore.
