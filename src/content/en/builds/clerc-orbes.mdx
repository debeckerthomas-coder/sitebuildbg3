# The Radiating Orb Cleric: The Aura of Blindness

*(1 Storm Sorcerer / 11 Light Domain Cleric)*

The "Radiating Orb" build is not here to deal the highest damage in the game, but to **break the boss math**. Every radiant damage you deal applies stacks of "Radiating Orb" in an area. Each stack inflicts a -1 penalty to the enemy's Attack Rolls. In a single turn, you can apply 10 stacks on a boss, rendering it unable to hit your party.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

We start with a Sorcerer level for purely min-max reasons: it grants proficiency in Constitution Saving Throws (crucial for maintaining Concentration on your spells) and access to the *Shield* spell.

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Elf (Wood or High) for bonus movement or sleep Immunity, or Duergar (for at-will invisibility).
- **Background:** Acolyte or Sage.

**27-Point Allocation:**

- **Strength: 8** (Unnecessary)
- **Dexterity: 14** (To maximise Armour Class with medium armour)
- **Constitution: 16** (Hit Points and unbreakable Concentration)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Dump stat)
- **Wisdom: 17** (Primary stat for Cleric spells. +1 from the Hag or a feat = 18)
- **Charisma: 10**

---

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

Your role shifts drastically from level 6 onward (Cleric level 5) when you unlock the *Spirit Guardians* spell. You become a divine "lawnmower."

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_lightcleric">

- **Level 1: Sorcerer (Storm Sorcery).** Why Storm? For repositioning. Casting a spell of 1st Level or higher lets you fly 3m (10ft) as a Bonus Action without provoking an Opportunity Attack (Tempestuous Magic). It is a short hop, not full flight, but useful for minor positioning adjustments. Take the <CodexTooltipById id="shield_spell" text="Shield" /> and <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" /> spells.
- **Levels 2 to 12: Cleric (Light Domain).** The Light Domain grants access to *Radiance of the Dawn* (a massive radiant AoE) and *Warding Flare* (a Reaction to impose Disadvantage on an enemy attack).
- **Level 5 (Cleric 4):** Take the **War Caster** feat. Combined with your Constitution proficiency, you will almost never lose Concentration on *Spirit Guardians*.
- **Level 9 (Cleric 8):** Take the **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Wisdom).
- **Level 11 (Cleric 10):** You unlock Divine Intervention. Save it for a legendary weapon or a full emergency heal on an endgame boss.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

Your strategy is not to kill enemies one by one, but to throw yourself into the thick of the melee to cripple their offensive potential.

1. **Turn 1 - Preparation:** Use your Action to cast *Spirit Guardians* (choosing the Radiant option, obviously).
2. **Reposition:** Thanks to your Storm Sorcerer level, casting *Spirit Guardians* lets you fly 3m (10ft) as a Bonus Action without provoking Opportunity Attacks (Tempestuous Magic). Use this short hop to nudge yourself into a better position among nearby enemies. Use your remaining movement to walk into the thick of the fight.
3. **The Domino Effect:** Every enemy within your *Spirit Guardians* aura takes damage. The <Rule term="armure_lumineuse">Luminous Armour</Rule> activates, creating a wave of Orbs. The Gloves activate. The Ring activates. This inflicts between 4 and 8 stacks of Radiating Orbs in an area (between -4 and -8 to their Attack Rolls).
4. **Turn 2 - The Flash:** Use your Channel Divinity *Radiance of the Dawn*. It is a 9-metre radius that ignores cover. Every enemy hit receives a fresh explosion of Orbs. The fight is mathematically won — they will do nothing but Critical Failures.
