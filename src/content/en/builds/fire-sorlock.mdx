# The Fire Sorlock: The Ardent Inquisitor

*(11 Draconic Sorcerer / 1 Fiend Warlock)*

The Fire Sorlock does the same thing as the Control Bard, but with spells instead of arrows — and it's arguably even more disgusting. One cast of *Scorching Ray* fires 4-5 rays. Each ray procs the Hat of Fire Acuity. In one action you're at max acuity stacks, and then you Quickened Spell a Command that literally cannot miss. Massive damage AND full board control in one turn.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Elf or Human (for shield proficiency).
- **Background:** Noble or Charlatan.

**27-Point Allocation:**

- **Strength: 8** - **Dexterity: 16** (Initiative and Armour Class)
- **Constitution: 14** - **Intelligence: 8** - **Wisdom: 10** - **Charisma: 17** (Primary damage stat. +1 from the Hag = 18)

---

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_firesorlock">

- **Level 1: Sorcerer (Draconic Bloodline - Fire).** Grants extra Hit Points, a base armour of 13, and proficiency in Constitution Saving Throws.
- **Level 2: Warlock (1).** The strategic multiclass. Choose the **Fiend** patron. This gives you access to the <CodexTooltipById id="command" text="Command" /> spell (essential for this build) and *Armour of Agathys*.
- **Levels 3 to 12: Sorcerer (2 to 11).** Return to Sorcerer for the rest of the progression.
- **Overall Level 4:** **Metamagic** (Quickened Spell and Twinned Spell).
- **Overall Level 5:** Take the **Alert** feat (Going first is vital).
- **Overall Level 6:** Unlock <CodexTooltipById id="scorching_ray" text="Scorching Ray" /> and *Fireball*.
- **Overall Level 7:** Draconic Elemental Affinity adds your Charisma modifier to EACH ray of your fire spells.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

1. **Action — light them up:** *Scorching Ray* (3rd or 4th level). 4-5 rays of fire, each one dealing CHA bonus damage from Draconic Affinity.
2. **Acuity spike:** Every ray procs the Hat. One spell = 10 stacks = +10 to spell DC. Your Spellmight Gloves add 1d8 per ray on top. The boss is melting.
3. **Bonus Action — lock the board:** Quickened Spell into <CodexTooltipById id="command" text="Command (Approach or Halt)" /> upcast on everything in sight.
4. **Result:** Massive single-target damage on the boss, and their entire entourage skips their next turn. Fight's over in one rotation.
