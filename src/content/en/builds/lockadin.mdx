# The Lockadin: The Herald of Twilight

*(7 Oathbreaker Paladin / 5 Pact of the Blade Warlock)*

Forget raw Strength. Forget Dexterity. The Lockadin is the **Main Character Syndrome** incarnate. By sealing an eldritch pact, this fallen Paladin bends reality to his will through a single ability score: **Charisma**. He is your party's face, your most resilient shield, and the most enduring Divine Smite engine in Honour Mode.

---

## 1. The Trinity of Charisma (The Build's Secret)

Why is this build the pinnacle of optimisation? Because it achieves the ultimate "SAD" (*Single Ability Dependent*) dream. Your Charisma handles absolutely EVERYTHING:

1. **The Word:** You pass every Persuasion and Intimidation check.
2. **The Blade (Pact of the Blade):** You use your Charisma instead of your Strength for Attack Rolls and Damage Rolls.
3. **The Aegis (Aura of Protection):** Your Charisma bonus is added to every Saving Throw for your entire party.
4. **The Heresy (Aura of Hate):** The Oathbreaker adds *yet again* his Charisma modifier to melee attack damage.

---

## 2. Character Creation & Ability Scores

Your deadliest weapon isn't your sword — it's your arrogance. We are dumping Strength entirely.

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Orc (Relentless Endurance and devastating critical hits) or Zariel Tiefling.
- **Background:** Noble, Charlatan, or Soldier.

**27-Point Buy Spread:**

- **Strength: 8** (Leave the dumbbells to the Barbarians)
- **Dexterity: 14** (For initiative and meeting medium/heavy armour prerequisites)
- **Constitution: 16** (Hit Points and Concentration)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Dump stat)
- **Wisdom: 10** (Just enough to avoid penalties)
- **Charisma: 17** (King stat. +1 from the Hag to reach 18 by Act 1)

## 3. The Leveling Odyssey (Level 1 to 12)

<EmergencyMatrix title="HONOUR MODE RULE (WARNING)" severity="warning">
  Unlike Tactician mode, in Honour Mode, the Paladin's Extra Attack (Lv 5) and the Pact of the Blade Warlock's Extra Attack (Lv 5) **do not stack**. You will only get 2 attacks per turn.
  *Then why play this build?* Because a level 5 Warlock grants you Level 3 Spell Slots that recharge on **Short Rests**. You can unleash devastating Divine Smites every single fight without ever needing to sleep!
</EmergencyMatrix>

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_lockadin">

- **Phase I — Levels 1 to 5:** Start as **Paladin (Oath of Vengeance)**. You wear heavy armour and hit (weakly for now) to survive.
- **Level 2:** Unlock <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> and the *Great Weapon Fighting* Fighting Style.
- **Level 4:** Take the **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Charisma).
- **Phase II — Level 6 (The Turning Point):** Multiclass into **Warlock (The Fiend)**. You sell your soul for power. Pick up the *Hex* spell.
- **Global Level 8 (Warlock 3):** Take the **Pact of the Blade**. Bind your weapon. Your weapon now uses your Charisma. The build officially "turns on".
- **Global Level 9 (Warlock 4):** Take the **War Caster** feat to never lose Concentration on *Hex* — or **+2 Charisma** if your CHA isn't at 20 yet.
- **Global Level 10 (Warlock 5):** You gain *Fireball* and your Warlock Spell Slots upgrade to Level 3 (Perfect for maxed-out Smites).
- **Phase III — Levels 11 to 12:** Return to Paladin up to level 7. *(Note: Aura of Protection only comes online at level 11 with this progression. Compensate with <CodexTooltipById id="shield_of_faith" text="Shield of Faith" /> and <CodexTooltipById id="sanctuary" text="Sanctuary" />.)*
- **The Act of Betrayal:** Break your Oath. Become an **Oathbreaker Paladin**.
- **Paladin Level 7:** Unlock the **Aura of Hate**. Your melee attacks deal additional damage equal to your Charisma modifier.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 5. The Symphony of Smiting (Combat Rotation)

You're not here for subtlety. You advance, and you obliterate.

1. **The Condemnation (Bonus Action):** On Turn 1, cast *Hex* on your target or use <CodexTooltipById id="vow_of_enmity" text="Vow of Enmity" /> if you haven't broken your oath yet. This instantly activates your Diadem of Arcane Synergy.
2. **The Execution (Action):** Strike with your two-handed weapon. You attack with Advantage (thanks to the Risky Ring).
3. **The Final Judgement:** You score a Critical Hit? The Reaction window opens. Spend a Level 3 Warlock Spell Slot to trigger a brutal <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" />.
4. **The Resilience:** At the end of combat, take a simple Short Rest. Your Warlock Spell Slots are restored. You are ready for the next slaughter.

## 7. Action Economy Management

A good Honour Mode player **never** wastes a Bonus Action. Here is your mental dashboard for every combat turn.

<EmergencyMatrix title="The Oathbreaker's Dashboard" severity="warning">
  **🟢 MAIN ACTION:**
  1. Attack with the Pact Weapon (Advantage via Risky Ring).
  2. Cast *Eldritch Blast* (If target is out of melee range).

  **🟠 BONUS ACTION:**
  1. Cast *Hex* (Turn 1) or transfer it to a new target.
  2. Cast *Vow of Enmity* (On a boss for Advantage if no Risky Ring).
  3. Bonus attack via *Polearm Master* (if equipped).

  **🔴 REACTION:**
  1. **Divine Smite!** (Set it to "Ask on every hit" in your spellbook to only burn your Lv 3 Spell Slots on Critical Hits).
  2. Opportunity Attack.
</EmergencyMatrix>
