# The Tavern Brawler Monk: The Damage Machine

*(8 Open Hand Monk / 4 Thief Rogue)*

The Tavern Brawler Monk is straight up broken. You chug an Elixir, your STR jumps to 27, and the Tavern Brawler feat doubles that modifier on every punch. The result? You punch things for 30+ damage per hit, 6 times a turn, with a 95% hit rate. Bosses melt. It's the highest single-target DPS in the game and it costs you one potion per Long Rest.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

Dump STR at character creation. Sounds crazy? You'll chug an Elixir of Giant Strength after every Long Rest anyway — free stats.

- **Optimal Race:** Wood Elf (for the movement speed vital for a monk) or Half-Orc (for survivability and critical hits).
- **Background:** Outlander or Folk Hero.

**27-Point Allocation (Elixir Mode):**

- **Strength: 8** (Replaced by the Elixir at 21 or 27)
- **Dexterity: 16** (Armour Class, Initiative)
- **Constitution: 14** (Hit Points)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Useless)
- **Wisdom: 16** (Open Hand bonus damage, Monk Armour Class)
- **Charisma: 10**

---

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

The multiclass into Thief Rogue is non-negotiable — you need that extra Bonus Action. More Bonus Actions = more Flurry of Blows = more punches = more dead things.

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_monk">

- **Levels 1 to 6: Monk.** Choose the **Open Hand** subclass at level 3 to unlock knockdown.
- **Level 4 (The Turning Point):** Take the **Tavern Brawler** feat with a point in Constitution. This feat adds TWICE your Strength modifier to your unarmed Attack Rolls and Damage Rolls.
- **Level 6:** Unlock *Manifestations* (adds Necrotic, Radiant, or Psychic damage to every hit).
- **Levels 7 to 9: Rogue (1 to 3).** At Rogue level 3 (overall level 9), choose the **Thief** subclass. You gain a permanent Bonus Action. For a Monk, a Bonus Action = a *Flurry of Blows* (2 attacks).
- **Levels 10 to 12: Monk (7 to 8) or Rogue (4).** The meta choice is to continue Monk to obtain *Evasion* and the level 8 feat (Wisdom Improvement +2).

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

You sprint across the map, reach the boss, and punch it into oblivion before it gets a turn.

1. **Pre-Combat:** Check your Elixir is active. Toggle a Manifestation (Radiant is best). Don't forget or you're leaving free damage on the table.
2. **Main Action:** Two standard punches. Knock them prone or apply debuffs.
3. **Bonus Actions — the blender:** Both Bonus Actions go into **Flurry of Blows**. That's 4 more punches.
4. **Result:** 6 hits in one turn. With 27 STR, Tavern Brawler, and Kushigo boots, you have a 95%+ hit rate on everything. The boss is either dead or one turn away from it.
