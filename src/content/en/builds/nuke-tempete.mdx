# The Storm Nuke: Elemental Annihilation

*(10 Storm Sorcerer / 2 Tempest Domain Cleric)*

The "Storm Sorcerer Nuke" relies on a devastating mathematical interaction. In BG3, the "Wet" status doubles Lightning and Cold damage taken. The Tempest Domain Cleric, through its Channel Divinity, allows you to skip the Damage Roll entirely and apply the maximum result instead. The result? A Chain Lightning that hits for 160 guaranteed damage on 4 targets simultaneously.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

We start as Sorcerer to gain proficiency in Constitution Saving Throws (to avoid losing Concentration on spells like <CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" />).

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Elf or Human (for shield proficiency if you are not wearing heavy armour), or Blue/Lightning Dragonborn (for thematic resistance).
- **Background:** Charlatan or Noble.

**27-Point Allocation:**

- **Strength: 8** (Useless)
- **Dexterity: 14** (Initiative and Armour Class)
- **Constitution: 16** (Hit Points and Concentration)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Dump stat)
- **Wisdom: 10** (Just enough to avoid penalties)
- **Charisma: 17** (Primary stat for your Sorcerer spell accuracy and damage. +1 via the Hag's Hair = 18)

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

The build takes a little while to come online, but becomes an absolute steamroller starting at level 7.

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_stormsorc">

- **Level 1: Sorcerer (Storm Sorcery).** You gain the ability to fly as a Bonus Action after casting a spell, granting you unparalleled mobility.
- **Levels 2 to 3: Cleric (Tempest Domain 1 & 2).** The immediate multiclass. You gain heavy armour proficiency and, most importantly, at Cleric level 2, **Destructive Wrath** (Channel Divinity): instead of rolling dice for a Lightning spell, you deal the maximum possible damage.
- **Levels 4 to 12: Sorcerer (Levels 2 to 10).** Return to Sorcerer for the rest of the progression.
- **Overall Level 5 (Sorcerer 3):** Unlock **Metamagic**. Take *Quickened Spell* to cast a spell as a Bonus Action, and *Twinned Spell*.
- **Overall Level 6 (Sorcerer 4):** Take the **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Charisma) or **Alert** (to always act first and nuke before enemies scatter).
- **Overall Level 10 (Sorcerer 8):** Take the **Dual Wielder** feat. This will allow you to wield TWO legendary magical staves simultaneously in Act 3.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

The objective is to set the stage with water, then press the nuclear button.

1. **Turn 1 - The Setup (Action):** Cast *Create Water* or throw a water bottle/barrel onto the group of enemies. They are now "Wet" (Vulnerable to Lightning).
2. **The Trigger (Bonus Action):** Use your Metamagic *Quickened Spell* to cast <CodexTooltipById id="chain_lightning" text="Chain Lightning" /> (or *Call Lightning* / *Lightning Bolt* if you are not level 12).
3. **Judgment Day (Reaction):** The game asks if you want to use your *Destructive Wrath*. Say YES.
4. **Mathematical Breakdown:** Chain Lightning deals 10d8 damage. Destructive Wrath locks the roll to 80. The Wet status multiplies by 2. **160 instant damage** to the primary target AND to three nearby targets. The fight is over.
