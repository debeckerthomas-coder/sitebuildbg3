# The Storm Nuke: Elemental Annihilation

*(10 Storm Sorcerer / 2 Tempest Domain Cleric)*

The "Storm Nuke" abuses a disgusting damage interaction that Larian never patched. In BG3, the "Wet" status doubles Lightning damage taken. The Tempest Cleric's Channel Divinity lets you skip the dice roll entirely and deal max damage instead. Stack these two together and you get a Chain Lightning that hits for **160 guaranteed damage on 4 targets at once**. No RNG. No counterplay. Just instant deletion.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

Start Sorcerer for CON save proficiency — you cannot afford to drop Concentration on <CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" /> or you're toast.

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Elf or Human (for shield proficiency if you are not wearing heavy armour), or Blue/Lightning Dragonborn (for thematic resistance).
- **Background:** Charlatan or Noble.

**27-Point Allocation:**

- **Strength: 8** (Useless)
- **Dexterity: 14** (Initiative and Armour Class)
- **Constitution: 16** (Hit Points and Concentration)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Dump stat)
- **Wisdom: 10** (Just enough to avoid penalties)
- **Charisma: 17** (Primary stat for your Sorcerer spell accuracy and damage. +1 via the Hag's Hair = 18)

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

The early levels are rough — you're basically a wet noodle until level 7. Then you press the nuke button and the game stops being fair.

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_stormsorc">

- **Level 1: Sorcerer (Storm Sorcery).** After casting a spell of 1st Level or higher, you can fly 3m (10ft) as a Bonus Action without provoking Opportunity Attacks (Tempestuous Magic). It's a short repositioning hop, not full flight — but it's free and it keeps you alive.
- **Levels 2 to 3: Cleric (Tempest Domain 1 & 2).** The immediate multiclass. You gain heavy armour proficiency and, most importantly, at Cleric level 2, **Destructive Wrath** (Channel Divinity): instead of rolling dice for a Lightning spell, you deal the maximum possible damage.
- **Levels 4 to 12: Sorcerer (Levels 2 to 10).** Return to Sorcerer for the rest of the progression.
- **Overall Level 5 (Sorcerer 3):** Unlock **Metamagic**. Take *Quickened Spell* to cast a spell as a Bonus Action, and *Twinned Spell*.
- **Overall Level 6 (Sorcerer 4):** Take the **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Charisma) or **Alert** (to always act first and nuke before enemies scatter).
- **Overall Level 10 (Sorcerer 8):** Take **Elemental Adept (Lightning)** to ignore Lightning resistance and treat any 1 on a damage die as a 2, or take a **+2 Charisma** boost to reach 20 and improve your spell save DC.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

Step 1: get them wet. Step 2: press the nuke button. Step 3: there is no step 3, they're all dead.

1. **Action — Soak everything:** *Create Water* or throw a water bottle/barrel on the pack. Everyone gets the "Wet" status (vulnerable to Lightning — damage doubled).
2. **Bonus Action — Nuke:** Quickened Spell into <CodexTooltipById id="chain_lightning" text="Chain Lightning" /> (or *Call Lightning* / *Lightning Bolt* pre-12).
3. **Reaction — Max it:** The game asks if you want to use *Destructive Wrath*. You slam YES.
4. **The math:** Chain Lightning = 10d8. Destructive Wrath = max roll = 80. Wet = double damage = **160 instant damage** on the primary AND three nearby targets. Fight's over before anyone else acts.
