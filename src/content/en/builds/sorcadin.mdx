# The Sorcadin: The Incandescent Aegis

*(6 Oath of Vengeance Paladin / 6 Draconic Sorcerer)*

The "Sorcadin" is the tankiest nuke in Honour Mode, and it's not even close. The idea is stupidly simple: Paladins need spell slots to fuel <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" />, and Sorcerers have a ton of them. Slap those two together and you get a walking war crime. But the real reason you go Paladin 6? *Aura of Protection*. Your entire party adds your Charisma to every saving throw. It's the most broken passive in the game and it's not even a contest.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

Unlike the Bardadin (Finesse/DEX), the Sorcadin goes full STR, slaps on the heaviest armour in the game, and swings a greatsword the size of a door.

- **Optimal Race:** Half-Orc (for Savage Attacks and Relentless Endurance) or Zariel Tiefling.
- **Background:** Noble or Soldier.

**27-Point Allocation (No Elixir):**

- **Strength: 16** (Primary attack stat)
- **Dexterity: 10** (Useless in Heavy Armour, let others handle Initiative)
- **Constitution: 14** (Hit Points)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Dump stat)
- **Wisdom: 8** (Aura of Protection will compensate for this penalty)
- **Charisma: 16** (For your spell save DC and Aura of Protection)

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

Start Paladin. No exceptions. You need Heavy Armour and Martial Weapons from level 1.

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_sorcadin">

- **Levels 1 to 6: Paladin (Oath of Vengeance).** Start Paladin for Heavy Armour and Martial Weapon proficiency.
- **Level 2:** Great Weapon Fighting style and <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" />.
- **Level 3:** <CodexTooltipById id="vow_of_enmity" text="Vow of Enmity" /> (guaranteed Advantage on a boss).
- **Level 4:** **Great Weapon Master** feat for the +10 to damage.
- **Level 5:** Extra Attack.
- **Level 6 (Crucial):** **Aura of Protection**. The defensive core of the build.
- **Levels 7 to 12: Sorcerer (1 to 6).** Choose the **Draconic Bloodline** (Fire or Silver) for survivability, or **Storm** for mobility.
- **Level 7 (Sorcerer 1):** Take the spells <CodexTooltipById id="shield_spell" text="Shield" /> (Armour Class to 25+ as a Reaction) and <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" />.
- **Level 9 (Sorcerer 3):** **Metamagic**. Take *Quickened Spell* to cast a spell as a Bonus Action and then attack.
- **Level 10 (Sorcerer 4):** Take the **Ability Score Improvement** feat (+2 Strength or +2 Charisma depending on your needs).

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

A normal Paladin has to choose: cast a control spell OR attack. The Sorcadin says "why not both?" and nukes the entire encounter.

1. **Bonus Action — Lock them down:** Quickened Spell into <CodexTooltipById id="hold_person" text="Hold Person" /> (or *Hold Monster*). The target is paralysed.
2. **Main Action — Walk up and execute:** Against a paralysed target, ALL melee attacks that hit are **guaranteed crits**. You walk up, you swing.
3. **The spike:** Both hits crit. The Reaction window pops up. You dump your highest-level *Divine Smites* into both. The damage is disgusting.
4. **Bonus kill:** Great Weapon Master procs on crit or kill — free Bonus Action attack. If you didn't Quicken this turn, save it for Turn 2 and do it all again.
