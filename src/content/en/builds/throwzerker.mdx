# The Throwzerker: The Heavy Artillery

*(5 Berserker Barbarian / 4 Thief Rogue / 3 Champion Fighter)*

The Throwzerker is one of the most popular Honour Mode builds for good reason: it's braindead easy and disgustingly effective. Berserker + Tavern Brawler + returning weapons = you're a siege turret that one-shots everything from range. The best part? Frenzied Throw knocks enemies Prone on hit — no saving throw, just land the attack. Free knockdown. Free advantage for your melee party members. It's completely busted.

---

## 1. Character Creation & Stats

Same Elixir cheese as the Monk — dump STR at creation, chug a potion, get 21-27 STR for free. Pump DEX for Initiative and WIS to resist enemy CC.

- **Optimal Race:** Shield Dwarf or Gold Dwarf (defensive racial bonuses, and perfect synergy with the *Dwarven Thrower* in Act 3), or Half-Orc.
- **Background:** Soldier or Criminal.

**27-Point Allocation (Elixir Mode):**

- **Strength: 8** (Replaced by the Elixir of Hill Giant Strength at 21 or 27)
- **Dexterity: 16** (Armour Class, Initiative, Saving Throws)
- **Constitution: 16** (Massive Hit Points)
- **Intelligence: 8** (Useless)
- **Wisdom: 14** (Vital mental Saving Throws)
- **Charisma: 10**

## 2. Leveling (Level 1 to 12)

You spike at level 4 — that's mid-Act 1. Everything after that is a victory lap.

<ChecklistTracker checklistId="leveling_throwzerker">

- **Levels 1 to 5: Barbarian.** Choose the **Berserker** subclass at level 3. This grants you *Frenzy Attack* and, more importantly, *Frenzied Throw* (throw an object/weapon as a Bonus Action).
- **Level 4 (The Turning Point):** Take the **Tavern Brawler** feat. Your throws gain TWICE your Strength modifier to Attack Rolls and Damage Rolls.
- **Level 5:** Extra Attack. You now throw twice per Action.
- **Levels 6 to 9: Rogue (1 to 4).** At Rogue level 3 (overall level 8), choose the **Thief** subclass to gain the additional Bonus Action. You can now make TWO frenzied throws per turn. At Rogue level 4, take the **Alert** feat (Initiative +5).
- **Levels 10 to 12: Fighter (1 to 3).** Gain the *Defence* Fighting Style (+1 Armour Class) and *Action Surge* at level 2. At level 3, take **Champion** to double your critical hit chance.

</ChecklistTracker>

## 4. The Death Loop (Combat Rotation)

**Always take the high ground.** Throwing from elevation adds bonus bludgeoning damage based on weight and fall distance. Free damage for smart positioning.

1. **Bonus Action #1 — Rage up:** Enter *Frenzied Rage*. You're online.
2. **Main Action — throw twice:** Hurl <Rule term="nyrulna">Nyrulna</Rule> or your Pike at the priority target. Two throws, two hits, massive damage.
3. **Bonus Action #2 — knock them down:** *Frenzied Throw* for a third hit. This one knocks the target **Prone** — no save, just hit them. Your melee allies now have free Advantage.
4. **Action Surge (boss fights):** Pop it for two more throws. That's 5 throws in one turn. Nothing survives 5 Tavern Brawler throws.
