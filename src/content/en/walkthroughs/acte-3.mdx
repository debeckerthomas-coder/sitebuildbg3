## I — The Race to Level 12: Baldur's Gate in Pillage Mode

Act 3 is a trap. The city of Baldur's Gate overflows with quests and exploration, but also with **impossible fights if you are not at maximum level**. Every Act 3 boss has Honour Mode mechanics that assume a party at the power ceiling.

> **CARDINAL RULE: DO NOT TRIGGER any boss fight before your entire party is Level 12.** Not 11. Not 11 and a half. **Twelve.** Level 12 unlocks level 6 Spell Slots (<CodexTooltipById id="globe_invulnerabilite" text="Globe of Invulnerability" />), the 3rd Extra Attack (Fighter), and final feats. Without them, you are under-equipped for every boss in this act.

### Peaceful XP Route (Lower City → Upper City)

The Lower City is a treasure trove of combat-free XP. Explore **everything** before triggering a single hostile scenario.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_xp_rivington" label="Explore Rivington completely — NPCs, merchants, side quests (~8,000 XP)" />

**Rivington** is Act 3's welcome zone. Talk to every NPC, resolve conflicts through diplomacy. The Tiefling refugee quest (Persuasion DC 12) yields 75 XP without combat. Akabi's circus offers 200 XP + legendary loot (see below). Rivington merchants sell <CodexTooltipById id="counterspell" text="Counterspell" /> Scrolls and Greater Healing Potions — **buy everything**.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_xp_lower_city" label="Explore the Lower City — cemetery, taverns, guild quests (~12,000 XP)" />

The **Lower City** is the densest XP zone in the game. Baldur's Gate Cemetery contains necromantic quests (~3,000 XP). The guilds (Thieves' Guild via Mol, the Counting House via the Zhentarim) offer entire narrative arcs resolvable without combat. Every tavern has at least one NPC with a quest. Expect ~12,000 XP from exploration and dialogue alone.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_xp_upper_city" label="Explore the Upper City — manors, diplomacy, political quests (~6,000 XP)" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_xp_undercity" label="Undercity — exploration + hidden merchants (~4,000 XP)" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_level12" label="Confirm: ENTIRE party is Level 12" />

**Total calculation:** Rivington (8,000) + Lower City (12,000) + Upper City (6,000) + Undercity (4,000) = **~30,000 XP** available without major combat. The Level 11 → 12 threshold requires ~14,000 XP per character. With the XP accumulated from Acts 1 and 2, this is more than sufficient.

<BuildTactic target="clerc_lumiere">
  **🛐 The Double Miracle (Light Cleric):** Descend into the secret basement of the Tabernacle (watch out for curse traps!). Open your deity's chest to steal the **Amulet of the Devout**. It increases your spell DC by +2 AND gives you an extra Channel Divinity charge. You can cast Radiance of the Dawn twice per combat!
</BuildTactic>

### Raiding the Sorcerous Sundries Vault

The Sorcerous Sundries Vault (Ramazith's Tower) contains the two most powerful Act 3 items for a spellcaster: the <CodexTooltipById id="markoheshkir" text="Markoheshkir" /> and the <CodexTooltipById id="robe_de_la_trame" text="Robe of the Weave" />.

#### The Door Puzzle — Complete Solution

The Vault is protected by a system of magically locked doors. Each room is a trap or puzzle. Here is the optimal route without combat:

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_vault_entree" label="Enter Ramazith's Tower — pass the guards (Persuasion DC 14 or Invisibility)" />

**Room 1 — The Lava Room:** The floor is covered in lava. Use <CodexTooltipById id="pas_brumeux" text="Misty Step" /> to teleport each character onto the raised platforms. Alternative: the Fly spell (level 3) on the whole party. **Do NOT walk through the lava** — 3d10 fire/turn, even with Fire Resistance that is 16.5 average damage/turn.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_vault_lave" label="Room 1: Cross the lava via Misty Step or Fly" />

**Room 2 — The Lever Puzzle:** Four levers control four doors. The correct sequence is: Lever 3 → Lever 1 → Lever 4 → Lever 2. An incorrect lever summons a Lava Golem (180 HP). In Honour Mode, the fight is doable but consumes resources. **Follow the sequence.**

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_vault_leviers" label="Room 2: Sequence Lever 3 → 1 → 4 → 2 — DO NOT make mistakes" />

**Room 3 — The Guardian Chamber:** An Elemental Guardian stands watch before the main vault. If you have a character with Stealth +9 or more, sneak past it (Guardian's passive Perception: 16). Otherwise, use <CodexTooltipById id="invisibilite" text="Invisibility" /> and steal the vault contents without engaging combat.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_vault_gardien" label="Room 3: Bypass the Guardian (Stealth or Invisibility)" />

**The Vault:** Contains the <CodexTooltipById id="markoheshkir" text="Markoheshkir" /> (legendary +3 staff) and the <CodexTooltipById id="robe_de_la_trame" text="Robe of the Weave" /> (+1 spell DC, +1 spell attack, permanent Mage Armour).

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_vault_markoheshkir" label="Retrieve the Markoheshkir — best staff in the game" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_vault_robe" label="Retrieve the Robe of the Weave — best caster armour" />

The <CodexTooltipById id="markoheshkir" text="Markoheshkir" /> is the best staff in the game. Its **Karsus Element** lets you choose an elemental damage type per Long Rest (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Thunder, Necrotic, or Force), and it grants <CodexTooltipById id="globe_invulnerabilite" text="Globe of Invulnerability" /> for free — a spell normally level 6 that consumes a precious slot. Against the <CodexTooltipById id="steel_watch_titan" text="Steel Watch Titan" /> (Lightning vulnerable), this automatically doubles all damage. Plan your Element based on the next boss.

## II — Dismantling Gortash's Empire

Gortash controls Baldur's Gate through two pillars of power: the **Steel Watch Foundry** (his golem army) and the **Iron Throne** (his underwater prison). Destroying both bastions BEFORE confronting Gortash himself is the optimal strategy — it eliminates his reinforcements and unlocks crucial allies.

### A — The Steel Watch Foundry

The Foundry is the factory producing the Steel Watchers — the metal golems patrolling Baldur's Gate. Destroying it is a major story objective, but the Foundry boss is the **Steel Watch Titan** — a giant golem with 300 HP that can crush a character in a single turn.

<BossCard id="steel_watch_titan" />

<EmergencyMatrix bossId="steel_watch_titan" />

#### Detailed Strategy — Steel Watch Titan

The Titan has **300 HP, AC 20, and a Lightning vulnerability**. This is your primary lever.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_fonderie_karsus" label="Set Markoheshkir to Lightning (Karsus Element) BEFORE entering the Foundry" />

**Lightning DPS Calculation:**
- <CodexTooltipById id="eclair" text="Lightning Bolt" /> (level 3): 8d6 = avg. 28, **×2 vulnerability = 56 damage**
- Lightning Bolt upcast (level 5): 10d6 = avg. 35, **×2 = 70 damage**
- Call Lightning (level 3, Concentration, 3d10/turn): avg. 16.5, **×2 = 33/turn**
- <CodexTooltipById id="markoheshkir" text="Markoheshkir" /> attack with Karsus Lightning: 1d8+3 bludgeoning + bonus Lightning damage, **×2 on the bonus**

**With two Lightning-specialised casters:** Turn 1 = 2× upcast Lightning Bolt = **140 damage**. Turn 2 = Call Lightning + Lightning Bolt = **103 damage**. Total in 2 turns: **243/300 HP**. The Titan is nearly dead by Turn 3.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_fonderie_t1" label="Turn 1: Double Lightning Bolt (upcast if possible) — exploit Lightning vulnerability" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_fonderie_prise" label="Prepare Misty Step or Freedom of Movement against Crushing Grip" />

> **MELEE TRAP:** The Titan's Crushing Grip grapples a character (STR Saving Throw DC 18) and deals 2d8+8 bludgeoning **automatically** each turn. A grappled martial loses their turn AND takes damage. The solution: <CodexTooltipById id="liberte_de_mouvement" text="Freedom of Movement" /> (grapple immunity) on your tank BEFORE combat, or <CodexTooltipById id="pas_brumeux" text="Misty Step" /> as a Reaction (teleportation breaks grapples).

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_fonderie_gondiens" label="Free the Gondian prisoners AFTER the fight — allies for the Iron Throne" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_fonderie_detruire" label="Destroy the Foundry Core — No going back (confirm)" />

### B — The Iron Throne (Underwater Prison)

The Iron Throne is Gortash's underwater prison. **Duke Ravengard** is held prisoner there. Rescuing him is mandatory for Wyll's narrative path and unlocks a powerful ally for the final battle.

> **5-TURN TIMER.** As soon as you enter the Iron Throne, a **5-turn countdown** triggers before the structure collapses. Every second counts. No optional combat, no exploration, no hesitation.

#### Mandatory Preparation — BEFORE Diving

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_trone_hate" label="Prepare Haste on 2 characters BEFORE entering — extra Action + doubled movement" />

<CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" /> is **non-negotiable** here. The spell doubles movement and grants an extra Action. A character under <CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" /> covers 18m/turn instead of 9m — the difference between reaching the Duke's cell on Turn 2 and reaching it on Turn 4 (too late).

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_trone_brumeux" label="Prepare Misty Step on the fastest character — teleportation through doors" />

<CodexTooltipById id="pas_brumeux" text="Misty Step" /> (18m teleportation as a Bonus Action) passes through **locked doors and walls**. The character under <CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" /> + <CodexTooltipById id="pas_brumeux" text="Misty Step" /> can reach the Duke's cell in **a single turn**: Movement (18m Hasted) → Misty Step (18m) → Dash Action (18m) = **54m total movement**. The prison is ~45m long. More than enough.

#### Turn-by-Turn Rotation

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_trone_t1" label="Turn 1: Fast character → Sprint to the Duke's cell. Rest of party → free nearby prisoners" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_trone_t2" label="Turn 2: Open the Duke's cell (Lockpicking DC 15 or Knock). Eliminate corridor guards if needed" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_trone_t3" label="Turn 3: Duke freed — EVERYONE sprints to the exit. No looting, no detours" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_trone_sanctuaire" label="Failsafe: Cast Sanctuary on the Duke if guards attack him during the escape" />

> **CRITICAL FAILSAFE:** If the Duke is targeted by guards during the retreat, <CodexTooltipById id="sanctuary" text="Sanctuary" /> (Bonus Action, level 1) protects him entirely — guards must succeed on a WIS Saving Throw to target him, otherwise they choose another target. This is your absolute safety net.

<FailsafeCard missing="Haste not prepared or Concentration lost" fallback="Speed Potion (identical effect to Haste, 3 turns) — purchasable from the Lower City merchant" condition="If the level 3 Spell Slot is already consumed or Concentration is broken" />

### BOSS: Gortash, Chosen of Bane (250 HP)

Gortash is the "cleanest" boss in Act 3 — a single enemy, a single deadly mechanic. He is the perfect test to validate that your party is ready for what follows.

<BossCard id="gortash" />

<EmergencyMatrix bossId="gortash" />

#### The Deadly Mechanic — Tyrannical Brand

**Tyrannical Brand** is the only thing that can kill you here. Gortash brands a character on Turn 1, and after **2 turns**, the curse explodes for **~110 force damage**. This is a one-shot on 95% of characters — only a Bear Totem Barbarian with 130+ HP would survive, and barely.

**Calculation:** A typical level 12 character has 80-100 HP (Martial with CON 16 = 12d10+36 = avg. 102 HP). 110 force damage = **guaranteed death** on any character with less than 111 HP. Force damage bypasses most resistances — there is NO Force Resistance in the base game.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_gortash_prep" label="Prepare Remove Curse on the Cleric BEFORE the fight" />

The solution is straightforward: <CodexTooltipById id="delivrance_maledictions" text="Remove Curse" /> purges the Brand **instantly**. Cast it **as soon as** the debuff appears — not next turn, not when it is "convenient". **Immediately.** The spell is level 3, range Touch — the Cleric must be adjacent to the branded character. Position your Cleric accordingly **before** the fight.

> **COMMON FATAL ERROR:** Forgetting to prepare <CodexTooltipById id="delivrance_maledictions" text="Remove Curse" /> on the Cleric because this spell is rarely useful in the rest of the game. Check your prepared spells. **Now.**

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_gortash_position_clerc" label="Position the Cleric within touch range of the likely Brand target" />

#### Turn 1 Execution — Hold + Burst

Once the Brand is purged, exploit Gortash's great weakness: he is a **humanoid**. <CodexTooltipById id="immobilisation_personne" text="Hold Person" /> paralyses him, guaranteeing **automatic critical hits in melee** as long as the paralysis holds.

**Turn 1 burst kill calculation:**

1. <CodexTooltipById id="immobilisation_personne" text="Hold Person" /> → Gortash paralysed (WIS Saving Throw +2 vs DC 17 = 75% paralysis with <CodexTooltipById id="casque_acuite_arcanique" text="Helmet of Arcane Acuity" /> active)
2. Paladin → Attack 1: 2d6+5 slashing (auto crit) + level 4 <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> (10d8 radiant crit) = **2×(7+5) + 2×(45) = 24 + 90 = 114 damage**
3. Paladin → Attack 2: same calculation with level 3 Smite = **~95 damage**
4. **Turn 1 total from a single Paladin = ~209 damage.** Gortash has 250 HP. A second attacker finishes the job.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_gortash_immo" label="Cast Hold Person as soon as the Brand is purged" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_gortash_burst" label="Maximum DPS — Level 4+ Divine Smite on guaranteed crits" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_gortash_kill" label="Finish Gortash in 1-2 turns" />

<FailsafeCard missing="Hold Person fails" fallback="Helmet of Arcane Acuity: each failed control spell applies -1 to Gortash's Saving Throws. After 3 attempts, his WIS Save drops from +2 to -1, making paralysis near-certain." condition="If the first Hold Person fails (25% chance)" />

### BOSS: Orin the Red, Chosen of Bhaal (200 HP → Slayer Form)

Orin is a cognitive trap. Her Phase 1 is trivial — a few poisoned daggers, minor cultists. The real fight begins when she transforms into the **Slayer** at 50% HP. And that is where runs die.

<BossCard id="orin" />

<EmergencyMatrix bossId="orin" />

#### Phase 1 — Human Orin (200 → 100 HP)

Phase 1 is a standard fight. Orin has 4 dagger attacks (+12, 1d4+5 piercing + 2d6 poison × 4), but her Poison immunity makes her cultists more dangerous than she is. Focus DPS on Orin to bring her to 100 HP as quickly as possible. Do not waste **any** level 5+ Spell Slots here — you will need them in Phase 2.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_orin_phase1" label="Phase 1: Bring Orin to 50% HP normally — save high-level spells" />

#### Phase 2 — The "Unstoppable" Mechanic (12 Invulnerability Charges)

At 100 HP, Orin transforms into the **Bhaal Slayer**. She immediately activates **Unstoppable**: each attack she receives is reduced to **1 damage** and consumes 1 charge. She has **12 charges**.

**The mathematical problem:**
- If you attack normally: 12 charges ÷ 2 attacks/character/turn ÷ 4 characters = **1.5 turns** just to exhaust the charges. But during this time, her Slayer Claws (2d8+6 × 3 = **avg. 45 damage/turn**) shred your party.
- In reality, with misses (AC 17), it is closer to **2-3 turns** for the charges. Unacceptable in Honour Mode.

> **THE SOLUTION: <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" />.** Each missile of <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" /> counts as a **separate attack**. Each missile consumes one Unstoppable charge. And <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" /> **never misses** — no Attack Roll, auto-hit.

**Optimal calculation:**
- <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" /> level 5 = **7 missiles** = 7 charges burned in one Action
- <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" /> level 6 = **8 missiles** = 8 charges burned in one Action
- Two castings (2 casters, or 1 caster + Scroll): 7 + 7 = **14 charges burned** → all 12 Unstoppable charges are exhausted with margin
- **Total time: 1 turn.** Compare to 2-3 turns with normal attacks.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_orin_missiles_prep" label="BEFORE the fight: Confirm 2 characters have Magic Missile prepared (level 5 or 6)" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_orin_missiles" label="Phase 2 Turn 1: Cast upcast Magic Missile (Level 5 or 6) × 2 characters" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_orin_burst" label="Phase 2 Turn 2: Charges exhausted → maximum physical DPS to finish the remaining 100 HP" />

> **ADVANCED TIP:** If only one character has <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" />, use a **Magic Missile Scroll** (level 5, purchasable from Lower City merchants ~400 GP) on a second character. Any class can use a scroll if they succeed on an Arcana check (DC 15). With INT 14+, that is 55% success rate.

<FailsafeCard missing="Magic Missile (neither spell nor scroll available)" fallback="Use multi-hit attacks: Scorching Ray (3 rays), Eldritch Blast (3 rays at level 11+), or bonus attacks + Action Surge. Slower but it works." condition="If no caster has Magic Missile prepared and no scroll is available" />

<BuildTactic target="gloom_assassin">
  **🩸 The Death Aura (Assassin):** Become an Unholy Assassin of Bhaal (accept Valeria's murder) to access the merchant Echo of Abazigal. Buy from him the **<Rule term="armure_bhaal">Bhaalist Armor</Rule>**. It makes all enemies within 2 metres VULNERABLE to piercing damage (damage ×2). With your daggers and point-blank bow, you will one-shot entire bosses.
</BuildTactic>

### OPTIONAL BOSS: Raphael, Archdevil (666 HP)

The hardest fight in the game. **666 HP**, <CodexTooltipById id="counterspell" text="Counterspell" /> as a Reaction (+8 to the check), massive fire AoE, and 4 Soul Pillars that make him nearly invincible. Only attempt this fight if you want the <CodexTooltipById id="helldusk_armour" text="Helldusk Armour" /> (21 AC, -2 reduction to all damage, Burning immunity).

<BossCard id="raphael" />

<EmergencyMatrix bossId="raphael" />

#### Rule 1 — Soul Pillars (ABSOLUTE PRIORITY)

4 Soul Pillars permanently strengthen Raphael: +2 AC, 20 HP/turn regeneration, resistance to all damage. **As long as a single pillar stands, Raphael is unbeatable.** Each pillar has ~40 HP — individually fragile, but there are 4 scattered across the arena.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_raphael_piliers" label="Turn 1: Destroy the 4 Soul Pillars — AoE (Fireball, Lightning Bolt) on pillar clusters" />

**Turn 1 Strategy:** Split your party. Two characters on the north pillars, two on the south pillars. Each Fireball (8d6 = avg. 28) can hit 2 pillars if you aim between them. Two Fireballs = 4 pillars destroyed in one turn. If you only have one AoE caster, use <CodexTooltipById id="eclair" text="Lightning Bolt" /> (line that passes through multiple pillars) as a complement.

#### Rule 2 — ABSOLUTE BAN on Radiant Damage Against Minions

> **DEADLY TRAP**: Raphael's demonic minions possess a **doubled fire damage reflection**. If you use <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> (Radiant) on a minion, the demon converts your damage to fire and reflects **double**. A level 3 Smite = 4d8 radiant → 4d8 × 2 = **8d8 fire reflected** = avg. 36 damage to YOUR attacker.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_raphael_radiant" label="NEVER use radiant damage on minions — Reserve Radiant for Raphael ONLY" />

**Inform the entire team BEFORE the fight.** A Paladin who reflexively Smites a minion can kill themselves in one hit.

#### Rule 3 — Globe of Invulnerability (Mandatory Phase 2)

In Phase 2, Raphael transforms into an Archdevil and unleashes his **Infernal Breath** (10d8 fire, DEX Saving Throw DC 20 = avg. **45 damage**). Against a grouped party, this is a **potential wipe** in a single turn.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_raphael_globe" label="Phase 2: Deploy Globe of Invulnerability — nullifies Infernal Breath and AoE ≤ level 5" />

The <CodexTooltipById id="globe_invulnerabilite" text="Globe of Invulnerability" /> nullifies any spell/ability of level 5 or less within its 3m zone. Infernal Breath is a level 5 spell — it is **completely neutralised**. Group your characters inside the Globe during Breath phases, exit to strike when Raphael is on cooldown.

**The <CodexTooltipById id="markoheshkir" text="Markoheshkir" /> grants the Globe for free** — you do not even have to consume a level 6 Spell Slot. This is the reason the Sorcerous Sundries Vault is listed before Raphael in this act.

<FailsafeCard missing="Globe of Invulnerability (Spell Slot used or Markoheshkir not retrieved)" fallback="Fire Resistance (level 3 spell) on the entire party — reduces Infernal Breath from 45 to ~22.5. Not ideal but survivable with 60+ HP." condition="If the level 6 Spell Slot is already consumed and the Markoheshkir is missing" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_raphael_kill" label="Finish Raphael — retrieve the Helldusk Armour (21 AC, -2 damage, Burning immunity)" />

<BuildTactic target="moine_bagarreur">
  **🥊 The Fists of God (Monk):** Save Hope and kill Raphael. She will give you the **Gloves of Soul Catching**. These gloves add 1d10 force damage to your unarmed attacks and heal you for 10 HP per hit. You are now invincible in 1v1.
</BuildTactic>

<BuildTactic target="lockadin">

</BuildTactic>

### OPTIONAL BOSS: Ansur, the Undead Dragon (400 HP)

Ansur is a bronze dragon turned undead, lurking beneath the Wyrmway below Wyrm's Rock. The fight is brutal but rewards with the two best defensive items in the game: <CodexTooltipById id="baldurans_giantslayer" text="Balduran's Giantslayer" /> and the <CodexTooltipById id="helmet_of_balduran" text="Helm of Balduran" />.

<BossCard id="ansur" />

#### The Central Mechanic — Stormheart Nova

Ansur possesses a unique ability: **Stormheart Nova**. When he takes flight and begins channelling, he is preparing to unleash a **massive AoE of 10d8 lightning damage** (avg. **45 damage**, DEX Saving Throw DC 18 for half) that covers nearly the entire arena.

**This is NOT a spell.** <CodexTooltipById id="counterspell" text="Counterspell" /> **does NOT work** against Stormheart Nova. It is an innate ability — no verbal component, no interruptible targeting.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_ansur_nova_detect" label="Detect the channelling — Ansur takes flight and glows with lightning (1 turn warning)" />

**You have 1 turn of warning.** When Ansur takes flight and begins to glow, the Nova arrives next turn. This turn is your survival window.

#### Nova Survival Strategy

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_ansur_globe" label="Option A: Globe of Invulnerability — completely nullifies the Nova" />

The <CodexTooltipById id="globe_invulnerabilite" text="Globe of Invulnerability" /> nullifies the Nova **if it is classified as a level 5 or lower ability**. In BG3, Stormheart Nova is treated as a level 5 spell — the Globe neutralises it entirely. Group your 4 characters inside the Globe during the channelling turn, then exit to strike.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_ansur_resist" label="Option B: Lightning Resistance on the entire party + Dash behind pillars" />

**If you do not have the Globe:** Lightning Resistance (spell, potion, or equipment) reduces the 45 damage to ~22.5. Combine with a Dash behind the furthest pillars. The pillars provide partial cover (+5 to DEX Saving Throw = 70-80% chance of halving = ~11 final damage with resistance + cover + successful Save).

> **ANSUR'S IMMUNITIES:** Lightning (obviously), Poison, Necrotic. **Vulnerability: Radiant.** Unlike Raphael's minions, radiant damage is **your best friend** here. <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> is doubled. The <CodexTooltipById id="sang_de_lathandre" text="Blood of Lathander" /> continues to blind undead — including Ansur.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_ansur_radiant" label="Maximum Radiant DPS — Divine Smite + Blood of Lathander (damage ×2 vulnerability)" />

**Radiant burst calculation:**
- Level 3 <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> + Undead bonus: 4d8 + 1d8 = 5d8 radiant = avg. 22.5, **×2 vulnerability = 45 per Smite**
- 2 attacks + 2 Smites in one turn = **90 radiant damage** from a single Paladin
- Ansur has 400 HP. In 4-5 turns of concentrated radiant DPS, he falls.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="acte3_ansur_kill" label="Finish Ansur — retrieve Balduran's Giantslayer + Helm of Balduran" />

<CodexTooltipById id="baldurans_giantslayer" text="Balduran's Giantslayer" /> (doubles Strength modifier on damage) and the <CodexTooltipById id="helmet_of_balduran" text="Helm of Balduran" /> (regenerates 2 HP/turn + anti-crit) make your tank **nearly immortal** for the remaining fights. These two items alone justify the risk of the Ansur fight.

## VI — The Elder Brain — Final Battle (Netherbrain — 450 HP)

This is the last fight of your Honour Mode run. Everything you have built — the builds, the equipment, the failsafes — converges here. The Elder Brain is not the hardest boss in the game (Raphael is worse), but its exclusive Honour Mode mechanic demands **absolute tactical discipline** that is tested nowhere else.

<BossCard id="netherbrain" />

### RED ALERT — EXCLUSIVE HONOUR MODE MECHANIC

> **RETRIBUTIVE IMMUNITY**: At the end of each round, the Elder Brain becomes **totally immune to ALL damage types** it suffered during that round, for the following round. If you mix Lightning + Radiant + Slashing on Turn 1, the Brain is **immune to all three** on Turn 2. A single character who attacks with the wrong damage type on the wrong turn **ruins the entire rotation**.

**Why this is devastating:** Imagine your Paladin strikes with a fire-enchanted sword on Turn 1 (the "Magical" Turn). The sword deals Slashing + Fire. The Brain becomes immune to Slashing AND Fire on Turn 2. On Turn 2 (the "Martial" Turn), your martials can no longer deal Slashing damage. Your rotation is **broken**.

<EmergencyMatrix bossId="netherbrain" />

### The Damage Segmentation Protocol

This is the most important strategy in the game. Every character must know **exactly** which turn they attack and which turn they simply buff/heal.

#### Turn 1 — Magical Damage ONLY

<SingleStepTracker stepId="brain_t1" label="Turn 1: Magical Damage ONLY (Lightning, Cold, Force)" />

On Turn 1, **only your spellcasters attack**. Allowed damage types:
- **Lightning**: <CodexTooltipById id="eclair" text="Lightning Bolt" />, Call Lightning
- **Cold**: Cone of Cold, Ray of Frost
- **Force**: <CodexTooltipById id="eldritch_blast" text="Eldritch Blast" />, <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" />

**Your martials on Turn 1:** They do **NOT** attack. They position, consume their <CodexTooltipById id="elixir_geant_collines" text="Strength Elixirs" />, cast <CodexTooltipById id="shield_of_faith" text="Shield of Faith" />, and prepare <CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" /> on Turn 2 attackers.

#### Turn 2 — Martial Damage ONLY

<SingleStepTracker stepId="brain_t2" label="Turn 2: Martial Damage ONLY (Radiant, Bludgeoning, Slashing)" />

On Turn 2, **your martials attack**. Allowed damage types:
- **Radiant**: <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" />
- **Slashing**: Weapon attacks (sword, axe)
- **Bludgeoning**: Weapon attacks (mace, hammer)

**Your casters on Turn 2:** They deal **no damage**. They heal, buff (<CodexTooltipById id="haste" text="Haste" />), or use non-offensive utility spells.

#### Turn 3+ — Alternate the Cycle

<SingleStepTracker stepId="brain_t3" label="Turn 3+: Alternate the cycle — back to Magical damage" />

The cycle repeats: Turn 3 = Magical, Turn 4 = Martial, etc. Each turn, the Brain loses the previous turn's immunities and gains those of the current turn. **Perfect** segmentation = 0 wasted turns.

#### THE MOST COMMON FATAL ERROR

> **REMOVE ALL ELEMENTAL ENCHANTMENTS FROM YOUR WEAPONS BEFORE THIS FIGHT.**

A character with an elemental weapon enchantment (e.g. +1d6 fire on a sword, <CodexTooltipById id="everburn_blade" text="Everburn Blade" /> with its passive 1d4 fire) who attacks on the "wrong" turn adds the Fire type to the Brain's immunity list. **Yes, even 1 fire damage is enough.** The immunity is binary — 1 fire damage = total Fire immunity.

**FORBIDDEN weapons on the Martial Turn:**
- <CodexTooltipById id="everburn_blade" text="Everburn Blade" /> (passive fire)
- Any weapon with Fire, Cold, Lightning, or Acid enchantment
- <CodexTooltipById id="nyrulna" text="Nyrulna" /> (thunder explosion = Thunder damage = Thunder immunity next turn)

**ALLOWED weapons on the Martial Turn:**
- <CodexTooltipById id="baldurans_giantslayer" text="Balduran's Giantslayer" /> (pure Slashing + Force bonus)
- +3 weapons without elemental enchantment
- <CodexTooltipById id="divine_smite" text="Divine Smite" /> (Radiant — allowed on the Martial Turn only)

### Phase 2 — Below 50% HP: Retributive Damage

Below 225 HP, the Brain deals **2d8 psychic** (avg. 9) to every melee attacker, **no Saving Throw**. This is NOT a Reaction — it triggers on **every hit**. A martial with 3 attacks per turn takes 3 × 9 = **27 automatic psychic damage per turn** just from attacking.

<SingleStepTracker stepId="brain_phase2_range" label="Phase 2: Switch to RANGED attacks exclusively" />

**Solution:** Switch entirely to ranged attacks.
- <CodexTooltipById id="eldritch_blast" text="Eldritch Blast" /> (Force, ranged, 3 rays at level 11+)
- <CodexTooltipById id="projectile_magique" text="Magic Missile" /> (Force, auto-hit)
- Bows and crossbows for martials (Slashing/Piercing)
- Weapon throws (<CodexTooltipById id="nyrulna" text="Nyrulna" /> returns automatically — perfect here, but beware of Thunder on the wrong turn)

<SingleStepTracker stepId="brain_phase2_dom" label="Prepare Freedom of Movement against Domination (WIS Saving Throw DC 20)" />

The Brain uses **Domination** (WIS Saving Throw DC 20) as a Legendary Action in Phase 2. A dominated character attacks your allies for 1 turn. A dominated Paladin with <CodexTooltipById id="great_weapon_master" text="Great Weapon Master" /> active can **one-shot your healer** in a single strike (2d6+15 × 2 = avg. 44 damage).

<CodexTooltipById id="liberte_de_mouvement" text="Freedom of Movement" /> before the fight immunises the most dangerous character (your main DPS) to Domination. For the others, stack Wisdom Saving Throw bonuses: Paladin 6 Aura of Protection (+CHA to Saves), Cloak of Protection (+1 Saves), Ring of Protection (+1 Saves).

<FailsafeCard missing="Freedom of Movement (slot used)" fallback="Paladin 6 Aura of Protection (CHA to Saves) + Cloak of Protection + Ring of Protection = +5-7 to WIS Saves. Against DC 20, with WIS +2 and bonus +6, you need a 12+ = 45% resistance." condition="If no level 4 slot is available" />

<SingleStepTracker stepId="brain_final_dps" label="Focus remaining DPS — alternate types until 0 HP" />
