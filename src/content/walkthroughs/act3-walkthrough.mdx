## The Wyrmway — Ansur, Undead Dragon

This is one of the hardest fights in Honor Mode. Ansur's **Stormheart Nova** can one-shot your entire party if you're not prepared. Read the emergency matrix below — every mechanic marked "LETHAL" **will** kill you if you ignore it.

<BossCard id="ansur" />

<EmergencyMatrix bossId="ansur" />

### Recommended Strategy

1. Pre-buff with <SpellTooltip id="haste">Haste</SpellTooltip> on your main striker
2. Open with <SpellTooltip id="divine_smite">Divine Smite</SpellTooltip> at max spell slot on your first crit
3. When Ansur begins channeling Stormheart Nova — **DO NOT** try to <SpellTooltip id="counterspell">Counterspell</SpellTooltip> it. It's an ability, not a spell
4. Instead: Dash all characters behind the farthest pillar or use Globe of Invulnerability

### Loot Checklist

After defeating Ansur, you unlock two of the strongest items in the game:

- <ItemTooltip id="baldurans_giantslayer">Balduran's Giantslayer</ItemTooltip> — doubles your STR modifier on damage. This is the reason we went Vengeance Paladin
- <ItemTooltip id="helmet_of_balduran">Helmet of Balduran</ItemTooltip> — 2 HP/turn regen + prevents crits against you

## The Final Battle — The Netherbrain

This is it. The culmination of your entire Honor Mode run.

<BossCard id="netherbrain" />

<EmergencyMatrix bossId="netherbrain" />

### Phase 1 Execution

- Use Vow of Enmity on the Netherbrain for guaranteed Advantage
- Stack <SpellTooltip id="divine_smite">Divine Smite</SpellTooltip> on every hit — you won't need spell slots after this
- Keep your squishiest party member **outside** 30ft range to avoid Psychic Blast
- If a party member gets **Dominated**, use the Sentinel feat reaction or Aura of Protection to mitigate

### Phase 2 — Retributive Immunity

**THIS IS THE MOST COMMON HONOR MODE DEATH IN THE GAME.**

When the Netherbrain drops below 50% HP, it gains Retributive Immunity. Every melee hit you land triggers **2d8 psychic damage back to you**. This is NOT a reaction — it triggers on every single hit.

Switch to ranged attacks, Throw builds, or spells. If you must melee, ensure Death Ward and Temp HP are active.
